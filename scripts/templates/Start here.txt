HoldMyRAM {{VERSION}} - quick start

1. Keep holdmyram.exe and holdmyram-helper.exe in the same folder.
2. Double-click holdmyram.exe. A tray icon appears (check the ^ arrow).
3. Click the tray icon to choose what to close. Default hotkey: Ctrl+Alt+R.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen): click
"More info", then "Run anyway". The program is not code-signed.
